Our Story

Rooted in the Land, Poured with Care

Treaty & Oak takes its name from the enduring symbols on our crest — the feather and the oak leaf, crossed like a handshake. It's a nod to the land this bar calls home, and to the kind of hospitality that rewards patience: slow-built cocktails, honest pours, and a room built for conversation.

Tucked inside TownePlace Suites Tecumseh, Treaty & Oak is the neighbourhood's quiet-luxury living room — a dark, velvet-lined retreat for travellers and locals alike.
